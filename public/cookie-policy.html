1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember information about your visit.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Session/login cookie (essential): keeps you logged in securely as you browse. Without this, you couldn't stay signed in to view your cart or orders. This cookie can't be turned off without logging out.

We also use your browser's local storage (not a cookie, but similar) to remember your light/dark theme preference — this stays only on your device and is never sent to our server.

3. Third-Party Cookies

When you check out, Stripe (our payment processor) may set its own cookies on the payment page to prevent fraud and process your transaction securely. These are governed by Stripe's own privacy policy, not ours.

4. Cookies We Don't Currently Use

This site does not currently use advertising cookies, third-party analytics/tracking cookies, or sell data to advertisers.

5. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Since we only use one essential cookie for login, blocking it will simply prevent you from staying signed in.
