Mihurma ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Order information: shipping address, phone number, and items purchased.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage information: pages visited and basic technical data (browser type, device) to keep the site working correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To let you track your order status in real time
  • To respond to customer service requests
  • To improve the store and prevent fraud

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what's necessary with:

  • Stripe — to securely process payments
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your order

4. Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies (like keeping you logged in) and a local preference (like your light/dark theme choice). See our Cookie Policy for details.

5. Your Rights

You can access, update, or request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us, or by editing your profile in your account dashboard.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard practices — encrypted password storage, secure sessions, and restricted admin access — to protect your information. No system can guarantee complete security, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your data.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@mihurma.com.
