Return Policy

Last updated:

📝 This is a general-purpose template. Adjust the timeframes and conditions below to match what you can actually offer, and check your local consumer-protection laws — many regions set a legal minimum return window regardless of store policy.

1. Our Promise

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, you can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange.

2. Eligibility

  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with tags attached.
  • You'll need your order number (found in your account dashboard).
  • Final sale, personalized, or intimate/hygiene items may not be eligible for return — this is noted on the product page when it applies.

3. How to Start a Return

  • Go to your Orders page and find the order
  • Contact us at hello@mihurma.com with your order number and reason for return
  • We'll send you return instructions and a shipping label if applicable

4. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund to your original payment method, typically within 5-10 business days. Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong or defective item).

5. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos — we'll cover the cost of return shipping and send a replacement or full refund.

6. Exchanges

Need a different size or color? Let us know when you start your return and we'll prioritize getting the right item to you.

7. Questions

Reach us anytime at hello@mihurma.com.
